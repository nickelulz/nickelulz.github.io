My nickname is Moofy, and I'm a (to-be) computational physicist. I write a lot of things for fun, including short stories, rants, and most importantly, fairly useless programs. I also listen to singers from the 90s and read books about useless and utterly helpless men.

![The Album Cover of Life by the Cardigans]({{ 'images/life.jpg' | relative_url }})

To-date, I've written (or an presently working on) a few programs (or libraries) that may be of interest to like-minded people, namely simulation libraries for various computational physics ends. The most important of these are:

1. [Experimentalis]({{ 'experimentalis' | relative_url }}), a small Python library for quickly, neatly, and expressively performing data analysis in Jupyter notebooks; intended largely for signal processing, modeling, or time-series forecasting.
2. [FINDS]({{ 'finds' | relative_url }}), a robust C and Python library for quickly performing and analyzing massive-scale passive hydrodynamics-based fish dynamics simulations with maximal efficiency.

This website is heavily inspired by a number of other people's work. I took the menu system and design philosophy from [lainchan.org](https://lainchan.org) and mixed it with the functional style closer to [tom7.org](http://tom7.org). As for the exact theme, this site is using the static site generator Jekyll with a heavily-modified version of the [Clyell theme](https://github.com/gildasio/clyell) by gildasio.
